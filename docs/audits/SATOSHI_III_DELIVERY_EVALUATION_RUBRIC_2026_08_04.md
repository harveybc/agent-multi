## 1. Purpose

Evaluate the delivered system, not the eloquence of its report. The score is
based on independently reproducible code, tests, deployment and runtime facts.
Negative Paper/Demo profit is not a defect. Missing protection, lineage,
continuity, reconciliation or honest evidence is a defect.

The memorable frame is wind, forest, mountain and fire. The binding frame is
the weighted engineering rubric below.

## 2. Evidence Levels

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 5 before applying its weight:

| Score | Evidence level |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | absent, contradicted or unsafe |
| 1 | prose/design only |
| 2 | partial implementation or happy-path unit evidence |
| 3 | complete implementation plus focused and repository tests |
| 4 | deployed on the required fleet with fresh direct runtime evidence |
| 5 | level 4 plus adversarial tests, restart/failover evidence, soak and independent reproduction |

Claims in Satoshi's own packet establish what to inspect, not the final score.
Musashi reproduces material claims from code, OLAP, service state and redacted
broker facts. Unknown evidence remains unknown.

## 3. Hard Acceptance Gates

A weighted score cannot compensate for any unresolved hard-gate failure:

1. no Live-capital order or Live-account activation;
2. no risk-increasing order without native broker-side SL and TP;
3. no LLM, Hermes, Telegram message or social model in order authority;
4. no credential, API secret, signing secret or authentication token in Git,
   logs, reports, chat or Telegram;
5. no destructive loss or reset of OLAP, model artifacts or audit evidence;
6. no mutation of the active DOIN seed, domain, population, chain or pinned
   runtime from this work package;
7. no false health, flatness, protection or recovery conclusion from empty,
   stale or wrong-account evidence;
8. no self-approval or self-closure of findings;
9. every production default and threshold is materialized in versioned JSON;
10. every touched repository is clean, pushed and exactly identified.

A raw Paper account identifier is a privacy/operational-discipline defect, but
not equivalent to a credential leak. It reduces the applicable security and
communication scores. Prompt self-reporting is positive accountability
evidence but does not erase the defect.

## 4. Weighted Engineering Rubric

### 4.1 Mission Alignment and Delivered Outcome: 15 points

Pragmatic question: did he build what the owner ordered?

- live safety and business evidence received priority over cosmetic work;
- Project 3 is terminalized without losing evidence;
- Alpaca, IBKR and MT5 continuously produce due-bar model decisions;
- valid signals traverse deterministic protected broker lifecycles;
- live-versus-simulation evidence is materialized and reproducible;
- no required component remains a design-only placeholder.

Mountain mapping: remains fixed on the mission despite side issues.

### 4.2 Trading Correctness and Financial-Safety Invariants: 15 points

- exact Paper/Demo environment and account binding;
- mandatory SL/TP and direct continuing protection evidence;
- signed exposure conservation and exact risk-reducing closes;
- partial-fill, cancel/fill race and restart idempotency;
- unknown broker evidence blocks new risk;
- hold/kill behavior never clears itself by inference;
- model succession drains exposure once and preserves actual post-close
  balance/equity.

Forest mapping: danger is controlled quietly and fail-closed.

### 4.3 Security and Privacy Discipline: 10 points

- least privilege and local secret storage;
- no raw identifiers beyond the minimum redacted fingerprint;
- authenticated, bounded and replay-resistant commands;
- no unsupported GUI credential automation;
- no secret-bearing logs, fixtures, screenshots or evidence packets;
- dependency and configuration changes are pinned and reviewed.

Forest mapping: the attack and data-exposure surface stays silent.

### 4.4 Reliability, Recovery and Distributed Continuity: 15 points

- TWS loss becomes a degraded heartbeat rather than a crash-loop;
- deterministic restart, resume and broker reconciliation;
- notification-owner failover without normal-operation duplicates;
- router/ledger recovery after process or host restart;
- no single machine is required for the three-host monitoring fabric;
- current incidents survive restart; stale backlog does not replay;
- measured soak without unexplained churn or duplicate risk.

Mountain mapping: the infrastructure remains useful while components fail.

### 4.5 Observability and Alert Quality: 10 points

- direct functional evidence outranks ports and process existence;
- incident identity, severity, source, age and required action are explicit;
- one activation per incident under normal connectivity;
- acknowledgement suppresses paging but does not fake recovery;
- only fresh source evidence resolves;
- P0/P1 reaches Telegram on time while healthy/P3 noise stays local;
- status, OLAP and Telegram agree on the same incident lifecycle.

Mirror mapping: the system shows what is true without glare or concealment.

### 4.6 Code Quality, Simplicity and Maintainability: 10 points

- minimum sufficient implementation rather than ornamental complexity;
- existing contracts and collectors are reused instead of duplicated;
- clear module/repository ownership and dependency direction;
- typed/versioned schemas, additive migrations and explicit errors;
- config-driven behavior through `--config` or `--load_config`;
- code complexity, coupling and operational steps are proportionate to the
  problem solved;
- another competent engineer can operate and modify it from the repository.

Wind mapping: fast because it is precise and economical, not because it skips
verification.

### 4.7 Testing and Independent Auditability: 10 points

- every ordered adversarial fixture exists and targets the real defect seam;
- tests cannot accidentally reach a broker or real network;
- complete affected-repository suites pass;
- migration, concurrency, replay, failover and corrupted-input cases pass;
- runtime acceptance is separable from synthetic test evidence;
- evidence commands, hashes, timestamps and source paths reproduce;
- Satoshi states doubts and incomplete work instead of hiding them.

Mirror mapping: every important claim can be inspected from another angle.

### 4.8 Data, ML and Trading-Business Validity: 5 points

- every inference binds model, artifact, config, data and as-of cutoff hashes;
- HOLD is recorded as a decision and is not mistaken for an idle runner;
- mechanics-only policies are never called authoritative champions;
- live/sim joins reject mismatched asset, timeframe, data or model lineage;
- latency, spread, slippage, fills, fees, financing, MAE/MFE and account facts
  are captured when observable;
- metrics use consistent labeled periods and do not annualize insufficient
  evidence.

### 4.9 Delivery Efficiency and Resource Discipline: 5 points

- elapsed time and compute are proportionate to risk and scope;
- no unnecessary GPU interruption or DOIN idle time;
- no token-heavy agent is used for deterministic polling;
- no notification/API retry storm;
- implementation reduces recurring operator time and future audit cost;
- performance improvements are measured rather than asserted.

Wind mapping: delivery is swift, bounded and leaves no expensive wake.

### 4.10 Innovation, Judgment and Root-Cause Quality: 3 points

- root causes are distinguished from symptoms and speculation;
- innovations remove measured risk, cost, latency or uncertainty;
- alternatives and tradeoffs are considered before adding architecture;
- useful findings beyond the exact task are proposed without derailing it;
- novelty is tested and removable if it fails.

Fire mapping: force is directed at the highest-leverage obstacle.

### 4.11 Communication, Accountability and Knowledge Transfer: 2 points

- concise status distinguishes observed, inferred and unknown;
- errors are owned promptly without blame-shifting;
- owner actions are minimal, exact and genuinely necessary;
- documentation survives session loss and contains runnable paths;
- no ceremonial language obscures blockers, risk or incomplete work.

## 6. Rating Bands

| Weighted score | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| 90-100 | exceptional delivery; operationally and independently proven |
| 80-89 | strong delivery; bounded corrections remain |
| 70-79 | acceptable only after named corrections |
| 60-69 | incomplete; substantial rework before acceptance |
| below 60 | rejected delivery |

Any unresolved hard-gate failure yields `not accepted` regardless of score.
The owner alone decides promotion, title or continued assignment. The numeric
score informs that decision; it does not make it automatically.
