# Continuous Research and Innovation Program

Status: active
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-07-31

## 1. Purpose

Papers P1-P5 convert current evidence into bounded contributions. They are not
the research horizon. This program continuously turns operational evidence,
negative results, reviewer objections, literature changes and business needs
into falsifiable research lines without allowing novelty theatre or an
unbounded idea backlog.

The academic role never becomes idle after a paper or audit. When no urgent
finding or manuscript gate is ready, it works the research queue in section 5.

## 2. Two Coupled Loops

### 2.1 Evidence-to-publication loop

```text
registered question
  -> verified related work
  -> discriminating experiments
  -> claim/evidence matrix
  -> reproducibility replay
  -> external review
  -> preprint/submission
  -> correction and maintenance
```

### 2.2 Discovery-to-program loop

```text
signal
  -> conjecture
  -> prior-art collision test
  -> falsifiable hypothesis
  -> cheapest decisive experiment
  -> research-line registration
  -> campaign or rejection
  -> paper, future-work item, replication or documented null result
```

Signals include incidents, OLAP anomalies, cross-asset behavior, unexpected
champions, simulator-to-paper discrepancies, protocol bottlenecks, new primary
literature and unsolved user/business constraints.

## 3. Horizon Portfolio

| Horizon | Window | Purpose | Capacity guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H0 | 0-3 months | Close evidence for active P1-P5 gates | 50% |
| H1 | 3-12 months | Run decisive experiments for registered P6+ lines | 30% |
| H2 | 12-36 months | Test high-risk theoretical or cross-domain ideas cheaply | 20% |

Capacity percentages guide academic and experimental attention, not machine
allocation. S0-S1 safety work preempts every horizon.

## 4. Research-Line Admission

A line enters the registry only when it has:

1. a falsifiable hypothesis;
2. an explicit closest-known prior-art search;
3. a measurable scientific contribution if supported;
4. a useful null result if rejected;
5. the cheapest experiment capable of changing the decision;
6. dependencies, ethical/security risks and a kill condition;
7. named academic and technical reviewers who do not self-approve.

Ideas remain `candidate` until the prior-art collision test is complete.
Compute-heavy work is forbidden before that test. A line is killed when the
claimed novelty already exists, required evidence is unlawful/unavailable, or
the decisive pilot contradicts it without a credible narrower hypothesis.

## 5. Permanent Satoshi Queue

Satoshi selects the first eligible item in this order:

1. verify or challenge an active S0-S2 finding;
2. verify citations and claims for the paper nearest an evidence gate;
3. run an adversarial reviewer simulation on an evidence-complete section;
4. perform a weekly primary-literature delta search for registered H1/H2 lines;
5. execute the prior-art collision test for the highest-value candidate line;
6. design a replication or cheapest decisive experiment;
7. audit manuscript corrections, retractions, licenses and disclosure policy;
8. synthesize quarterly program decisions and retire weak lines.

Every task is bounded by source count, question count, elapsed time or output
schema. No task authorizes runtime mutation, broker action, paper submission or
claim promotion.

## 7. Prioritization

Lines are ranked as a Pareto portfolio, not by one synthetic score. The review
records:

- information value and cheapest falsification;
- scientific novelty after verified related work;
- business and cross-domain leverage;
- dependency readiness;
- implementation and compute cost;
- security, legal and data risk;
- probability that a null result remains publishable/useful.

P16 is the first technical research priority because a bounded model can find
protocol counterexamples without campaign mutation. P6 follows with a replay
of the measured 8.42% aggregate tail-barrier idle in three complete
generations. P7, P9, P13 and P19 receive bounded collision tests because they
reuse existing evidence. P14 is deferred until real inference traces exist;
P17 and P18 remain H2 until their assumptions and formal definitions are
credible. P20 is admitted from the owner's historical NEAT observation after
a primary-literature collision test; its broad curriculum mechanism is prior
art, while the narrower chronological insolvency design remains a falsifiable
candidate contribution. It cannot consume campaign GPUs or alter a
materialized domain before termination telemetry and the CPU pilot exist.

For the active ETH program, owner priority temporarily places P20-P23 and the
P9/P12 live-data children ahead of unrelated H1 work. The curriculum decision is
first. Accepted research variants are confirmed and frozen on ETH before
cross-asset optimization. P23 receives one bounded feasibility decision but may
not hold the stack open after its kill/defer condition. Portfolio P10 begins
only after the owner-selected per-asset library is complete.

## 7.1 ETH-to-Portfolio Integration (2026-08-06)

Document 33 is the active execution ordering for P9, P10, P12 and P20-P23:

```text
curriculum decision
  -> bounded ETH feature/learning/representation/synthetic pilots
  -> joint confirmation and mature ETH contract freeze
  -> one final full ETH optimization and reference champion
  -> representative second-asset transfer
  -> selected per-asset DOIN campaigns and champion library
  -> portfolio optimization
```

This order deliberately pays the model/data/preprocessing research cost once on
ETH before multi-day per-asset campaigns. A cross-asset transfer failure can
change the shared contract only through one bounded correction and reconfirmation;
it cannot create silent bespoke pipelines that make portfolio cells incomparable.

## 8. Cadence and Outputs

- Per incident/champion: consider one registry signal, with deduplication.
- Weekly: literature delta and one registry decision.
- Monthly: H0/H1 dependency and experiment review.
- Quarterly: retire, split or promote lines; publish an internal synthesis.
- At every preprint: register at least two evidence-linked future-work items.
- Annually: correction/retraction sweep and book-outline revision.

The authoritative registry is
`docs/publications/RESEARCH_LINE_REGISTRY_2026_07_31.csv`. Registry history is
append-only in Git; state corrections cite evidence and never erase rejected
ideas.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

The program is functioning when:

1. every active line has a next falsifiable task or a documented blocker;
2. no compute-heavy experiment begins before prior-art collision review;
3. negative and killed lines remain queryable;
4. Satoshi always has one bounded, non-runtime task available;
5. claims and future-work statements point to registry IDs;
6. quarterly reviews show promotions and retirements, not backlog growth alone.
