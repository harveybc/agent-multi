## Interpretation And Next Action

This pass does not establish a global feature-count limit. It establishes
resistance to single/coherent-block removal in the69-column starting context,
followed by three beneficial historical readmissions under the changed actor.
The resulting72-column context has not yet had its own deletion pass. Frozen
donor rejection is also not proof against matched retraining or other feature
interactions. Do not label72 as the minimum or optimal number of inputs.

controls. Next refresh TRAIN same-policy native continuation targets and
calibrate the new72-input critics before bounded refinement. A later floating

return pool (now48 absent features); do not repeat the unchanged69-input pass.
