# 382: development-only short closer factorial

Four fits: position population (constructed early/late opens from flat vs
shorts on the frozen 379 table trajectory) x 24-decision continuation (source
vs scalar short closer). Annual native episodes, identical opener and native
order terms, no new feedback. For all deployed arms and the scalar continuation
use the unchanged 379 long table. The original source target resumes the
entire original source policy as in 379. The scalar short teacher is the scalar
component of the constructed/source fit on 2020-21, not a scalar fitted on
2022. Same 379 table architecture, loss, penalty, strict positive CLOSE.

Per fit-year quarter preselect up to 128 flat builder origins on the frozen
trajectory (seed 382), try opening a short now or at the next decision.
Keep intact positions at decision three. Match counts to reachable ticks
without replacement within the decision quarter, capped at 96 per arm and
quarter. No outcomes used for inclusion. Never cross the annual episode
boundary when labeling. Fit both targets on identical states within population.
Freeze all four heads before evaluating any of them in 2022. Evaluate all
reachable short ticks on the same frozen 379 trajectory, then replay each
complete account separately. Controls: unchanged 379 table and the earlier-fit
scalar short teacher. No hyperparameter search or 2023+ accounts. Inherited
source, behavior policy and incumbent already saw 2022: this holds out the
year for the four new short heads, not for the whole system.

Evaluate on both targets; primary local metric is mean regret in initial
stop-risk units against the scalar continuation. Pair deltas by calendar
week. Report account net, drawdown, buy/sell and wins; distinguish a local
improvement from an account improvement. A follow-up candidate needs lower
regret (paired 95% week-block interval below zero vs constructed/source),
better full-account net than constructed/source and both controls, and
drawdown no worse than constructed/source. This descriptive gate does not
promote any policy: only one later year and four related arms.
