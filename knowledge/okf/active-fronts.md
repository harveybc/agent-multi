Front 1: distributed protected-entry optimization campaign (one
collaborative DOIN swarm, one chain at a time). Front 2: continuous
selected-model Paper/Demo execution on Alpaca, IBKR and OANDA MT5 with
mandatory native SL/TP — the flagship mission. Front 3: bounded social
intelligence, human-gated publishing. Front 4: independent audit and
continuous improvement under the role-swap protocol. Front 5: multi-domain
program documented and gated (doc 30); execution awaits gates G1-G4.
Fresh runtime truth always comes from heartbeats, ledgers and the
watchdog, never from this concept.
