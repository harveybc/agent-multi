The owner holds final authority over activation, capital, risk increases,
legal matters, publication and finding closure. Deterministic services
make and execute every runtime decision; LTS is the sole order authority.
No LLM, Hermes, GBrain or knowledge tool ever places, sizes, cancels or
approves an order, selects a model for a live seat, mutates a campaign,
publishes externally or closes a finding. Implementer and verifier are
always different parties. Missing facts are refusals, never zeros or
successes. Live capital is not authorized anywhere in the current plan.
