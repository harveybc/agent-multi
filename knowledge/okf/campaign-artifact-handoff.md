The fleet executes one collaborative DOIN swarm, one seed and one chain at
a time; a startup barrier refuses a second swarm rather than risking
parallel lineages (doc 15). Champions and elite archives are materialized
with exact artifact, config, data and code hashes. A simulation champion
reaches a Paper/Demo seat only through the doc 32 v1.1 gate: independent
52-week promotion panel, paired weekly RAP with a deterministic
moving-block bootstrap, frozen comparison family, three-seed stability,
seven-day zero-order shadow (operational evidence only) and a
flat-boundary drain/reseed handover.
